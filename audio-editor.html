Welcome

Hello, and welcome. My name is Mobeen, and I built this app for you. This app lets you edit audio and video simply by typing a command, like talking to an assistant. This tutorial explains the buttons on the screen, the app's features, and how to give commands using your Gemini API key. You can close this tutorial at any point.

Buttons and Their Locations

  • Play Tutorial button, top-left corner of the screen. Opens this tutorial.
  • API Key button, top-right corner, just below the browser's "more options" menu. Tap it to enter your Gemini API key. It stays on screen even after you save a key.
  • Tap Upload Audio / Video on the first screen to start. Supported formats: Audio — MP3, WAV, M4A. Video — MP4, WEBM, MOV, up to 80MB.
  • Just above the phone's home button, one row of buttons, left to right:
    • Download — saves your edited audio or video.
    • Attach — adds an audio, video, or image file. You can select several files at once. The button's name updates automatically: "Attach/Upload 1st Audio/Video," then "Attach/Upload 2nd Audio/Video," and so on.
    • Rewind — moves playback back a few seconds.
    • Play — plays or pauses the current audio or video. This is the only Play button in the app, and its position never changes.
    • Forward — moves playback ahead a few seconds (fast forward).
    • Edit Box — tap this to open the command box and give an editing instruction.

How the App Works

Tap the Edit Box button. All other buttons will hide, and a text box with a Send button will appear. Type your instruction, for example "cut the first ten seconds" or "add background music from zero to thirty seconds", and tap Send. The app will process your command using your Gemini API key. Once processing is complete, the box closes and the regular buttons return.

You can give more than one instruction in the same message. You can also mark a position while listening, by saying something like "mark this point" while paused, and later give another instruction referring back to that point; the app remembers it.

If you have attached extra audio or video files, you can ask the app to play any of them by name, like "play the second audio". It starts playing automatically using the same Play button, and you can rewind or fast-forward it just like the main track.

If the app doesn't fully understand your command, it will ask you a short question instead of guessing. Simple commands like Play, Pause, Undo, and Redo happen right away without asking for confirmation; anything that changes your audio or video will first show you what it understood and ask "Yes, do it" before making the change.

On audio, you can ask for: cut/trim, deleting a part, silencing several places, merging or mixing in another file, volume, fade in/out, reverb, echo, bass/treble, pitch, speed, normalize, compressor, reverse, and sound effects. When mixing in music, say where it should start and end — it will fit exactly into that range. On video, you can ask for: cut/trim, deleting a part, silencing, merging another video, mixing in a song, muting, volume, fade in/out, and speed. Say "play the original" any time to compare with the untouched version using the same Play button.
